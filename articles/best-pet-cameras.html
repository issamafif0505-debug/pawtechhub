🔑 What to Look for in a Pet Camera

  • Resolution: 1080p is the minimum standard. 2K gives noticeably sharper detail — worth it for identifying what exactly your pet is chewing on.
  • Night Vision: Color night vision (Furbo, Wyze) is vastly superior to infrared green. Look for "color night vision" specifically.
  • Treat Tossing: Essential for dog owners. Less impactful for cats — many cats ignore the treats entirely, so don't overpay for the feature if you only have cats.
  • Subscription Cost: Calculate total 2-year cost. A "cheap" $35 camera with a $10/mo subscription costs more than a $69 camera with $7/mo after 2 years.
  • Field of View: 130°+ or rotating. Fixed cameras with less than 130° will leave blind spots in most rooms.
  • Separation Anxiety: Two-way audio is proven to reduce anxiety in dogs. Look for cameras with echo cancellation for clear sound.

📌 Final Verdict

For most dog owners, the Furbo 360° is the clear winner — rotating camera, treat tossing, bark alerts, and color night vision in one package. On a budget? The Wyze Cam v4 delivers 2K quality for under $36 with free local storage. Privacy conscious? Go Eufy — zero subscription, forever.

Whatever you choose, being able to check in on your pet during a long day is priceless. 🐾
