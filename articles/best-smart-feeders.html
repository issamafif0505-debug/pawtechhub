🔑 How to Choose the Right Automatic Feeder

  • Portion Size: Indoor cats need 5–20g per meal. Large dogs may need 2+ cups. Verify the feeder's min/max portion matches your pet's diet plan.
  • Hopper Capacity: A 3L feeder lasts ~1 week for one cat. For a large dog, you need 6L+ to make it through a long weekend.
  • Anti-Jam Design: Cheaper feeders clog with certain kibble shapes. The PETLIBRO's rotor and PETKIT's screw mechanism are the most reliable we tested.
  • Power Backup: If you travel, battery backup is non-negotiable. Power outages happen — only PETLIBRO has this on our list.
  • Dry Food Only: All feeders on this list handle dry kibble only. For wet food, look for refrigerated feeders (different category entirely).
  • Dog vs Cat Bloating: If your dog eats too fast, PetSafe's Slow Feed mode is genuinely life-saving. Bloat (GDV) kills dogs — take it seriously.
