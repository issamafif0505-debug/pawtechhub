🔑 How to Choose the Right GPS Tracker

When choosing a GPS tracker for your dog, consider these key factors:

  • Battery Life: Ranges from 7 days to 3 months. Longer is better for convenience.
  • Tracking Type: True GPS (cellular) vs Bluetooth (Apple AirTag). GPS is more reliable for real-time tracking.
  • Subscription Costs: Most trackers require monthly plans ($5–$13/month).
  • Durability: Look for waterproof (IPX7+) and durable designs.
  • Size & Weight: Choose a tracker that suits your dog's size and breed.
